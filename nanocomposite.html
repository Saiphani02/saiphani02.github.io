CNT/epoxy nanocomposite: how much stiffer?

Five percent carbon nanotubes doubles the stiffness of epoxy. The textbook upper bound says it should be nine times higher.

What

Find out how much stiffer epoxy gets when you add short carbon nanotubes, and how close the standard formulas come to a full finite element answer.

How

A Python script in Autodesk Fusion builds a 10 mm epoxy cell with aligned nanotubes (length 10 times the diameter) at 0, 1, 3 and 5% by volume, placed at random with no overlaps, and exports STEP. In ANSYS Mechanical each tube is bonded to the epoxy, the cell is meshed with quadratic tets and stretched 0.1% along the tubes. The reaction force gives the effective modulus.

Results

At 5% CNT the modulus goes from 3.0 to 6.07 GPa, and FEA stays within 5% of Halpin-Tsai at every fraction. The rule of mixtures predicts 52 GPa. Short tubes only carry full load near their middle, so length matters more than the amount you add.
