Ball-and-beam: pole placement vs LQR

Two controllers, one ball: speed vs smoothness.

ControlsFall 2026Course project, EEL 5613 Modern Control
MATLABSimulinkState spaceLQRPole placement

What

Hold a ball at a set position on a tilting beam, and compare two state-feedback designs.

How

Newtonian model, linearized to state space. Pole placement and LQR in MATLAB/Simulink, an LQR weight study, then both controllers run on the full nonlinear plant.

Results

LQR reaches the 0.1 m step about twice as fast, but swings the beam to about 6° against 2° for pole placement and needs a sharper starting torque.

Both controllers tested on the full nonlinear plant, not only the linear model
