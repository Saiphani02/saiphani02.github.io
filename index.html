About me

The engineer

I started in automobile engineering, then spent time on the shop floor at Deccan Auto, checking bus body assemblies for tolerance and mechanical faults. After that I interned as a design engineer at Eron Infoways, building CAD models and running FEA before parts went to prototype.

At FAU I moved deeper into analysis: finite element methods, computational mechanics, flight dynamics, modern control and optimization. The work I enjoy most is setting a model up properly, checking it two ways, and using it to answer a practical question. My next step is robotics, where all of that meets real hardware.
