Semi-active suspension with an MR damper

A new damper control law that keeps the comfort and grip and uses less suspension travel.

What

Find a controller for a magnetorheological damper that keeps the ride comfortable without losing tyre grip or using up suspension travel.

How

Quarter-car model (290 kg body, 40 kg wheel) built from script in Simulink, with a Bouc-Wen damper model and a 20 ms coil lag. Four published controllers against a new frequency-split law, 126 runs on ISO 8608 roads, comfort scored with ISO 2631.

Results

The new law matches the best published hybrid on comfort and grip and needs 3 to 32 points less peak travel. On a class B road: 7.7% better grip than passive with 17.6% less travel. No semi-active law beat a well-tuned passive damper on both at once.

126 runs: 3 road classes × 2 random seeds per setting
Ideal active LQR used as a reference ceiling
Limits stated: quarter car, scaled damper data, one speed
